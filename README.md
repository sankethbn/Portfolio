## Projects

**[E-Commerce Sales Dashboard (Power BI)](https://github.com/sankethbn/E-Commerce-Sales-Dashboard-using-Power-BI)**
Broke down e-commerce sales by quarter, product, and profit margin — the kind of view I'd want open during a business review instead of digging through a raw export.

**[Amazon India Sales Analysis (Tableau)](https://github.com/sankethbn/Amazon-Sales-Analysis)**
A Tableau workbook on Amazon India's sales numbers — quantity, revenue, and category performance sliced by week so the patterns actually show up instead of hiding in a spreadsheet.

**[Global Performance Dashboard (Tableau)](https://github.com/sankethbn/Global-Performance-Dashboard-using-Tableau)**
Built to give leadership one view of sales and profit performance across regions and categories, instead of five different reports.

**[Email Campaign Analysis (Power BI)](https://github.com/sankethbn/Email-Campaign-Analysis)**
Dug into email marketing campaign data — delivery, opens, clicks, rejections — to see what was actually working and what wasn't.

**[Sales Performance Analysis (Excel)](https://github.com/sankethbn/Sales-Performance-Analysis-using-Excel)**
An Excel dashboard tracking a sales team's numbers — earnings, top performers, calls made, enrollments — month over month.

**[Customer Churn Analysis](https://github.com/sankethbn/Customer-Churn-Data-Analysis)**
Looked at why telecom customers were leaving, what actually correlated with churn, and what the business could do about it (spoiler: contract type and monthly charges mattered a lot more than I expected going in).
